Welcome to Pawsitive Rescue

At Pawsitive Rescue, we believe every animal deserves a second chance and a loving home. Our nonprofit rescue organization is dedicated to helping abandoned, neglected, and homeless animals find safety, care, and the families they deserve. Through the support of passionate volunteers and a caring community, we work every day to rescue animals in need and guide them on the journey toward their forever homes.

Since our founding in 2016, Pawsitive Rescue has helped more than a thousand animals begin a new chapter in their lives. From energetic dogs and curious cats to small companions like rabbits, each animal that comes through our doors is treated with compassion, patience, and respect. Whether you’re looking to adopt a new best friend, volunteer your time, or support our mission, you are an important part of helping us create brighter futures for animals.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple but powerful: rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in need while promoting responsible pet ownership throughout our community. We provide medical care, training, and loving foster environments for animals who have been abandoned or mistreated. By working closely with volunteers, adopters, and local supporters, we strive to give every rescued animal the opportunity to thrive in a safe and loving home.
