Upcoming Events

Pawsitive Rescue is proud to bring the community together through fun events, volunteer opportunities, and seasonal fundraisers. These events help us connect adoptable pets with loving families while raising support for the animals in our care.

Date Event Location Description
October 19 Fall Adoption Fair Pawsville Community Park Meet adoptable pets, visit local vendors, and enjoy food trucks during one of our biggest community events of the season.
November 2 Volunteer Orientation Pawsitive Rescue Center Learn about volunteer roles, training, and the many ways you can support our rescue efforts.
November 16 Pet Photos with Santa Pawsville Mall Bring your pets for a festive holiday photo and help raise funds for local rescue animals.
December 7 Winter Donation Drive Pawsitive Rescue Center Help us collect blankets, pet food, toys, and other supplies to keep rescued animals warm, safe, and comfortable during the winter season.
