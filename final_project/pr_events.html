Upcoming Events

Pawsitive Rescue is proud to bring the community together through adoption fairs, volunteer events, and seasonal fundraisers. These events help support rescue animals while connecting them with loving families and supporters.

November 16 Pet Photos with Santa Pawsville Mall Bring your pets for festive holiday photos while helping raise funds for rescue animals and shelter care.
