About Pawsitive Rescue

Pawsitive Rescue was founded in 2016 by a small group of passionate animal lovers who believed every abandoned or neglected animal deserved a second chance. What began as a foster-based rescue effort quickly grew into a nonprofit organization focused on rescue, rehabilitation, and rehoming for animals in need.

Over the years, Pawsitive Rescue has helped more than 1,000 animals find loving forever homes through community support, volunteer efforts, and compassionate care. We work closely with foster families, veterinarians, and local supporters to ensure every rescue animal receives the medical care, socialization, and attention they deserve while waiting for adoption.

Beyond rescue work, our organization is dedicated to promoting responsible pet ownership and community education. Through outreach events, volunteer programs, and local partnerships, we strive to create a safer and more compassionate future for animals throughout the community.
